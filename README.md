# Pokemon: Ancient Pink
*Welcome to the quiet village of Taeku, a peaceful settlement in the Suihou region. The people of Taeku worship a fickle deity, glimpsed so rarely that it seems to be a myth. All that can be confirmed from the few sightings are a flash of pink that darts between the trees, and mysterious bubbles of light that float into the sky after sunset.*

*One fateful night, you, the child of the village elder, have a mysterious dream: a voice calls out to you, whispering that you are fated to help save Taeku from impending danger. As you wake, you discover a peculiar object beside you: a ball, carved from an Apricorn, with a pale pink exterior. Within lies the mysterious deity that protects Taeku, calling to you to assist it...*

---

Such is the basis of **Pokemon: Ancient Pink**! Follow the journey of the child of Taeku's village elder, Seido/Akagane, as they explore the wilds of the ancient Suihou region, guided by the fickle yet benevolent guardian deity of Taeku, **Mew**. Gather wild Apricorns of various colors to craft new Pokeballs, form bonds with other settlements, and discover the threat that Mew warned of in your dreams...

## Major Differences from Normal Games
- **No Pokemon League:** There is no established Pokemon League, meaning there is no Gym challenge to take on. There are a handful of trainers, but the idea of battling Pokemon for sport hasn't spread very far yet, meaning they don't pay out any sort of prize money.
- **Paid Healing:** Pokemon Centers aren't a thing here, and instead villages have pharmacies and apothecaries who can treat all of your Pokemon, but their services cost money. After all, medicine isn't cheap.
- **Pokemon Brokers:** Because there aren't many trainers and they don't even provide money after victory, money is harder to come by. However, Pokemon can be valuable aides for different jobs, so villages are willing to buy Pokemon from you. Some of them may request specific types, or even specific species, but they'll pay even more if you bring them what they need!
- **No PC Storage:** Mew will handle storing your excess Pokemon using their powerful psychic abilities, meaning you can switch Pokemon at any time.

## Features
- **Item Crafting:** Gather supplies to create Pokeballs, medicine, and other essentials to travel through the wilds of Suihou. Your bag can only hold so much, but the **Crafting Kit** your parent grants you at the start of your journey fits comfortably inside no matter what. Learn recipes from those you meet in your travels, and even from Mew, observing your creations!
- **In-Game Day-Night Cycle:** The in-game clock doesn't follow real time, meaning less waiting for supply nodes to restore themselves and less waiting for specific Pokemon species to come out!
- **Exploration Aided by Mew:** As you travel through Suihou, you'll inevitably come across barriers that block your way. Of course, you also happen to have a "partner" who can help you with every one of them, so long as you can impress Mew enough to get them to help.
- **Suihou/Hisui Regional Variants:** Over 50 Pokemon have Suihou regional variants, and thanks to being rather close to the Hisui region, a handful of Hisui Pokemon can be found as well!

## Important Characters
- **Mew:** The fickle, playful deity worshiped by the village of Taeku. They hide from anyone they don't deem worthy of their attention, but when they need to solve a problem and require human aid, they'll make sure that their chosen human can get the job done.
- **Village Elder:** Seido's mother/Akagane's father, the elder of Taeku who acts as a priest(ess) at the shrine dedicated to Mew. They receive periodic premonitions of disaster, but because they are unable to leave Taeku unattended, it falls to Seido/Akagane to resolve these disasters.
- **Mitsuri:** Seido/Akagane's neighbor, whose parents run a small apothecary. She sells medicinal blends to assist Seido/Akagane during their travels, and heals their Pokemon. Unlike other villages, Mitsuri will heal them for free.
- **Kohaku:** A wandering merchant who travels from village to village to trade. His stock of items is random and supply is limited, but he'll always make sure to provide the best quality to his patrons. Just don't ask him where he gets some of them.
