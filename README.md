# Pokemon: Ancient Pink
*Welcome to the quiet village of Taeku, a peaceful settlement in the Suihou region. The people of Taeku worship a fickle deity, glimpsed so rarely that it seems to be a myth. All that can be confirmed from the few sightings are a flash of pink that darts between the trees, and mysterious bubbles of light that float into the sky after sunset.*

*One fateful night, you, the child of the village elder, have a mysterious dream: a voice calls out to you, whispering that you are fated to help save Taeku from impending danger. As you wake, you discover a peculiar object beside you: a ball, carved from an Apricorn, with a pale pink exterior. Within lies the mysterious deity that protects Teakru, calling to you to assist it...*

---

Such is the basis of **Pokemon: Ancient Pink**! Follow the journey of the child of Taeku's village elder, Seido/Akagane, as they explore the wilds of the ancient Suihou region, guided by the fickle yet benevolent guardian deity of Taeku, **Mew**. Gather wild Apricorns of various colors to craft new Pokeballs, form bonds with other settlements, and discover the threat that Mew warned of in your dreams...

## Features
- **Item Crafting:** Gather supplies to create Pokeballs, medicine, and other essentials to travel through the wilds of Suihou. Your bag can only hold so much, but the **Crafting Kit** your parent grants you at the start of your journey fits comfortably inside no matter what. Learn recipes from those you meet in your travels, and even from Mew, observing your creations!
- **No Pokemon League:** Unlike conventional games, *Ancient Pink* does not involve any sort of Pokemon League. That means no Gym Badges to gather!
- **Exploration Aided by Mew:** As you travel through Suihou, you'll inevitably come across barriers that block your way. Of course, you also happen to have a "partner" who can help you with every one of them, so long as you can impress Mew enough to get them to help.
- **Suihou/Hisui Regional Variants:** Over 50 Pokemon have Suihou regional variants, and thanks to being rather close to the Hisui region, a handful of Hisui Pokemon can be found as well!
